# User guide

A step-by-step guide to using NoChickenCall on a trip.

## 1. Get the app
1. Open **https://nochickencall.onrender.com** on your phone.
2. Add it to your home screen:
   - **iPhone:** in Safari, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
   - **Android:** tap **Install app** on the home page (or Chrome menu → *Install app*).
3. Open NoChickenCall from the home screen icon from now on.

> On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate logins, so log in once inside the installed app.

If the app hasn't been used for a while, the first screen can take up to a minute while the free server wakes up. You'll see **"Waking up the server…"**.

## 2. Create your account
1. Tap **Create account**, then enter:
   - **Display name:** what friends see on trips. You can change it any time.
   - **User ID:** what you log in with; friends see it as @yourid. **It can't be changed later**, so pick carefully. IDs that look like admin or staff accounts are reserved.
   - **Password:** at least 8 characters.
2. **Save your recovery code.** There's no email or phone on your account, so this code is the only way to reset your password. Copy it into your password manager or Notes, tick the box, and tap **Let's go**.

**Forgot your password?** On the log-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and enter your username, recovery code and a new password. You'll get a **new** recovery code. Save that one too.

**Account screen** (tap your avatar, top right): profile photo, name, password, new recovery code, app status, log out.

## 3. Start a trip and invite friends
1. On **My trips**, tap **＋ New trip**.
2. Give it a name, pick the **base currency** (all balances and the final settle-up use it, e.g. SGD), and optionally add dates. Tap **Create trip**.
3. Tap **＋ Invite people** → choose expiry/limit if you like → **Create invite link**.
4. Share it: **Share…** (WhatsApp, Telegram…), **Copy link**, or let friends scan the **QR code**.

Friends open the link, create an account (or log in), and tap **Join**. They then appear under **People**.

> You can also paste a link on **My trips → 🔗 Join a trip**.

## 4. Add expenses
On the trip page, tap **＋ Add expense**.

1. **Amount + currency.** Pick the currency you actually paid in. For a foreign currency, the app shows the day's rate and the converted amount:
   - **Use my own rate** if your bank gave you a different rate.
   - If the trip owner set a **fixed rate** (Settings), it's used automatically.
2. **What was it for?**, the **category** and the **date**. The category is picked for you from what you type ("Grab to airport" → 🚕 Transport, "晚餐" → 🍜 Food & drinks); tap another one to change it.
3. **Paid by:** tap who paid. If several people paid, tap **Several people** and enter each amount until it says **✓ Adds up**.
4. **Split**:
   - **Equally:** tick who shares it.
   - **Amounts:** type each person's exact amount.
   - **%:** type percentages that add up to 100.
   - **Shares:** use − / ＋ (e.g. a couple = 2 shares, singles = 1).
5. Tap **Save**.

If the signal drops while saving, you'll see **"Not sure it saved"**. Just tap Save again; it can't be added twice.

**Filter by category:** on the trip's **Expenses** list, tap a category (🍜 Food & drinks, 🚕 Transport, 🏨 Stay, 🎟️ Activities, 🛍️ Shopping, 🛒 Groceries, 🧾 Fees & bills, 💵 Cash / loan, 📦 Other) to see only those and their total.

**Edit or delete:** tap an expense → **Edit** / **Delete** (you can change expenses you added; the trip owner can change any). The expense's **History** shows every change.

## 5. Check balances
- The trip page shows the **trip total** and **you get back / you owe**.
- **Who owes who**, also on the trip page, shows right now who should pay whom and how much (e.g. *Bob owes Alice S$40.00*). It updates live as expenses are added, so you can check it any time during the trip.
- **Who owes who → Balances** lists everyone's paid amount, share and net position:
  green = gets money back, red = owes, grey = settled up.

## 6. Settle up
1. Anyone can open **Settle up** on the trip page to preview **who pays whom**. It uses the fewest transfers possible, marked **Minimum possible**.
2. When the trip's over, the **owner** taps **Finish trip & settle up**. Expenses lock.
3. **If you owe:** pay in your banking app (PayNow, bank transfer…). Tap **Copy** to copy the exact amount, then tap **I've sent it**.
4. **If you're owed:** when the money arrives, tap **Got it ✓**. If it hasn't arrived, tap **Not received** and add a note. The payer can mark it sent again; if the money turns up anyway, you can still tap **Got it ✓**.
   - **Owner:** if a payment was sorted out another way (cash, or swapped against another debt), tap **Settled another way…** and add a note. It counts as paid.
5. Watch the progress (e.g. *3 of 5 transfers confirmed*). When every transfer is confirmed, you'll see **🎉 All settled!**

**Forgot an expense after finishing?** The owner can **Reopen trip** on the Settle up screen. Payments already received stay counted (listed under **Paid earlier**), and the remaining transfers are recalculated when the trip is finished again.

**Why do I pay someone who didn't pay for my stuff?** To keep transfers to a minimum, debts are combined. For example, you might pay Alice even though Bob paid for your taxi. The totals always work out exactly.

## 7. Trip settings (owner)
Open the trip → **⚙︎ Settings**:
- Rename the trip, change dates or description. The base currency can only change before any expenses exist.
- **Fixed exchange rates** for the whole trip. These never change expenses already saved.
- **Delete trip:** only when everyone's balance is zero. Type the trip name to confirm. It disappears for all members.

## 8. Using it without signal
With no signal (e.g. on a plane or a remote beach), the installed app still opens and shows your trips, expenses, balances, **who owes who** and the settle-up plan as they were last loaded. A banner says **“Offline · view only · Last updated 3:42 pm”**.
- Trips listed on *My trips* are downloaded in the background while you're online, so they're ready offline.
- Adding or changing anything needs a connection; those buttons say “needs a connection”.
- Logging out removes the offline copy from the phone.

## 9. Updates
When a new version is out, you'll see **"New version ready · Refresh"** at the bottom. Tap **Refresh**. It won't interrupt you while you're typing.

## Invite a friend
Account → **🎁 Invite a friend** shows your personal link. Share it; when a friend creates an account with it, you **both get 1 month as 💛 Supporter**. Each extra friend adds another month (up to 12 months ahead). Supporters help keep the app free; Supporter extras are coming.

## Admin
The app has one admin account (`admin`). It can only be created with a private one-time code set on the server.
- Tap the 🛡️ button (top right) to see **all trips** with a status tag:
  - 🔵 **Active:** created, no transactions yet (invites / pre-trip).
  - 🟢 **On-going:** within the trip dates, or has transactions.
  - 🟣 **Needs finishing:** the end date has passed but the owner hasn't tapped Finish.
  - 🟠 **Pending settlement:** finished; not every payment is confirmed yet.
  - ⚪ **Ended:** finished and fully settled.
  - 🔴 **Deleted:** deleted by the owner.
- Switch to the **Users** tab to see every account: display name, @ID, when they joined and were last active. Search by name or ID, and tap a person to see the trips they own or are in (with each trip's tag). Tap a trip to open it.
- Matching a payment: search **Users** for the `NCC-…` reference from the Telegram message or bank record, then tap **Lifetime**.
- In **Users**, tap a person to set their **Supporter** status (**+1 month**, **Lifetime**, **Remove**) and see who invited them.
- **Disable account…** (Users → tap a person): only once they're settled in every trip. They can still view their trips but can't change anything, and see a message to PM @mashpotatas. Find them under **🚫 Disabled**, where **Enable account** undoes it. A trip with a disabled member can't be reopened.
- **Delete permanently…** on a trip in the list removes it and everything in it for everyone, forever (for test trips). Type the trip name to confirm.
- Opening a trip you're not in is **read-only**. In trips you've joined, you're a normal member.

## FAQ

**"Account disabled"?** Your account can only view trips for now. PM [@mashpotatas](https://t.me/mashpotatas) on Telegram to find out why.

**"This trip can't be reopened"?** Someone in the trip has a disabled account. PM @mashpotatas on Telegram for more info.

**Why is a payment marked "sent" not counted yet?**
Only the person receiving the money can confirm it. Until then, balances don't change.

**Why does my rate differ from my bank's?**
The suggested rate is an official daily reference rate. Use **Use my own rate** to enter your bank's rate instead.

**Who can see my profile photo?**
Only you and people you share a trip with.
